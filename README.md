# FlashCards

A small spaced-repetition app for words. Cards, tags, and review history stay on the device.

## Study

A new word is shown word first, and it stays that way while it is on a short learning step (1 minute, then 10 minutes). After it is scheduled for tomorrow or later, reviews alternate word-first and translation-first by successful review streak. Missing a review sends it back to the short steps, including later in the same lesson.

Again, Hard, Good, and Easy set the next due time. The progress bar counts a word only when that time is at least a day away.
